The SSH Terminal's Herdr and Rex buttons (bottom bar, menu, and Bottom Bar
settings) draw these marks as single-color template images.

# Herdr
Assets.xcassets/herdr-logo.imageset is Herdr's logo, cropped to the head and
horn, with its background tile removed, and converted from SVG to PDF.
Herdr is licensed under the Apache License 2.0, which grants no rights to its
marks; the logo remains Herdr's and names the program the button opens.

Source: https://github.com/ogulcancelik/herdr/blob/main/assets/logo.svg
Project: https://herdr.dev

# Rex
Assets.xcassets/rex-logo.imageset is an original glyph made for Oppi, after
the dinosaur of Rex's app icon: generated with Qwen-Image 2512 on this
project's own hardware, then traced to vector. It is not Superlogical's
artwork. Rex is a product of Superlogical, which does not endorse Oppi.

Project: https://www.superlogical.com/rex/docs/
