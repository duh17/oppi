Oppi's file icons and chat tool rows show the Godot logo for GDScript, Godot
scene and resource files, and Godot shaders (Assets.xcassets/lang-godot.imageset).
It is drawn in one color, with the eyes, nose and mouth band cut out, and
converted from SVG to PDF.
