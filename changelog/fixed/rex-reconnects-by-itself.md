- **Client:** A Rex terminal whose connection drops (the network goes away, Wi-Fi changes, the SSH connection under it closes) no longer waits for a **Reconnect** tap. It says it is reconnecting and comes back by itself, continuing the same Rex client and each pane's stream where they stopped, so the screen carries on with nothing reloaded; what you type meanwhile goes once it is back. See [A Rex terminal that loses its connection](docs/ssh-terminal.md#a-rex-terminal-that-loses-its-connection).
