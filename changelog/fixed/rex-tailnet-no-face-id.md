- **Client:** A saved host's Rex pane opened over Tailscale no longer asks for Face ID or the passcode on its own. When its connection drops, it comes back over Tailscale only, and it reads the host's other Rex panes over Tailscale, as Rex's own apps do. SSH, and its Face ID approval, is used only for something you start. See [Reaching Rex over Tailscale](docs/ssh-terminal.md#reaching-rex-over-tailscale).
