- **Client:** On iPad, **Back** in a terminal that the chat's Terminal button or the pane menu opened in its own pane now closes that pane and ends the terminal's connection. Before, Back left the pane open on a "Connecting to …" page that never finished, and the terminal's connection stayed open behind it. Opening the terminal again starts a fresh connection. On iPhone, or when the terminal opens over the chat, Back still returns to the chat and keeps the terminal running for the next time you open it.
