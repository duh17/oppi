- **Client:** On a wide iPad window, every pane Oppi opens for you lands by one rule. The chat's **Terminal** button and Rex terminal links now open below the chat in both orientations, so the shell gets the window's width; before, the terminal opened to the right as a tall, narrow column. Files, folders, web pages, games, and a workspace's sessions open to the right in landscape and below in portrait. Each reuses a pane already showing the same kind of thing, or an empty **New Pane**, and opens on the other side when only that side has room. Godot, sessions, and Rex terminal links open beside the chat instead of over it. A new **Settings → Panes** page, listed on a wide window, sets **Automatic**, **Right**, or **Below** for **Terminal**, **Files**, **Web**, **Games**, and **Sessions**. **Open in New Pane to the Right** or **Below**, a drop on a pane's edge, and **Split Right** or **Split Down** still go where you pick. iPhone and narrow windows are unchanged. See [Split panes](docs/usage.md#the-ipad-side-column).
