- **Client:** **All Sessions** is the first row of the sidebar (the iPhone's drawer), and the sidebar and drawer highlight the place you are in; tap **All Sessions** to come back to every session from Agents, a workspace, or any other place. On iPad in landscape, a workspace's session list no longer has its own back chevron to All Sessions. See [Screen map](docs/usage.md#screen-map).
- **Client:** On iPhone and on iPad in portrait, a place you pick in the drawer, open from an `oppi://pins` link, or open with a keyboard shortcut now replaces the screen you were on instead of covering it: a chat that was open does not stay under Pinned. Back from the place goes to **All Sessions**. See [Screen map](docs/usage.md#screen-map).
