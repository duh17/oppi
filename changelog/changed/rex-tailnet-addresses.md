- **Client:** Rex over Tailscale takes more than `*.ts.net` names: a Headscale tailnet's MagicDNS name over HTTPS, and a Rex listening on its host's own Tailscale (`tailnet://:7297`) at its tailnet address or `*.ts.net` name, which Oppi learns from the host's Rex or you add in **Terminal Settings → Rex on Your Tailnet**. Plain HTTP goes only to a tailnet address or a `*.ts.net` name. See [Reaching Rex over Tailscale](docs/ssh-terminal.md#reaching-rex-over-tailscale).
