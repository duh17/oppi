- **Client:** On iPad, Back returns to where you came from in landscape too. A chat opened from a session list, Pinned, Agents, or Schedules slides in over that screen instead of replacing it, keeps the sidebar button, and Back goes back to that screen; rotating the iPad no longer changes where Back goes. Workspace Settings has Back instead of Done. A terminal that is the first screen of a pane beside the chat shows **Close** (✕) instead of Back and closes only that pane. See [Screen map](docs/usage.md#screen-map).
