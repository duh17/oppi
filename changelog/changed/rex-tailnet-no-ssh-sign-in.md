- **Client:** A saved host whose Rex Oppi reaches over Tailscale now opens its Rex list and Rex panes with no SSH sign-in, password, or Face ID, as Rex's own apps do, and a dropped SSH connection no longer ends a Rex pane that runs over Tailscale. A pane signs in to SSH only when something must run on the host, such as an upload. See [Reaching Rex over Tailscale](docs/ssh-terminal.md#reaching-rex-over-tailscale).
