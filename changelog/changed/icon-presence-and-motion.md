- **Client:** A chat's session icon is a 40 pt circle in the top bar, and tapping the title anywhere, the icon or the name, opens the session's menu: the whole name, **Copy Session ID**, **Rename**, **Share Session**, and **Edit Agent** (**Pi Settings** for a session launched without one). A new chat led by an Agent opens on that Agent: its icon on a large circle with its name and description, and the composer saying **Message** and its name; the first message flies the circle into the bar's own, or cross-fades with motion off. A session launched without an Agent keeps the plain Pi mark on its empty chat. Icons now play one short moment instead of staying dead still: when a chat opens, when a turn ends, and when the session needs you (a question, an approval, a failure), reusing the burst session rows already play.
- **Client:** One **Settings → Chat → Motion** setting, **Full** (default), **Moments only**, or **Off**, decides how much moves anywhere in the app: the busy spinner, the dictation indicator, the sidebar's mark, every icon, and the icon moments. **Moments only** keeps the loops still and still plays the moments; **Off** draws every icon on its first frame. Reduce Motion, Low Power Mode, or Auto-Play Animated Images off holds everything still. The **Busy Animation** and **Dictation Animation** rows still choose how a spinner or a listening control looks; Motion decides whether it moves.
