- **Client:** On iPad, a file opens at the width it reads best at. A reader beside a chat or the **Files** list, a commit's diff, and a file opened in a new pane to the right now take the width that fits lines of about 75 characters at your text size, and the list or chat keeps the rest; before, a reader opened at a fixed width (about 65 characters in landscape) and a file pane took half the window. Rendered Markdown and Org files cap their lines at about 75 characters instead of about 90. Where the two do not fit side by side (the iPad mini in portrait, or in landscape with the sidebar showing), the file still covers the list or chat, which stays a strip you tap to bring back. iPhone is unchanged.
