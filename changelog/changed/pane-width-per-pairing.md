- **Client:** On iPad, the width you drag a side column to is remembered for that kind of column: a reader beside a chat, **Files** or **Git** beside a chat, and a file beside the **Files** list each come back at their own width, also after a relaunch. Before, one width served every column, so narrowing **Files** also opened the next reader narrow. Double-tap the divider to give the column its default width back.
