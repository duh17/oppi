- **Client, Server:** Durable sessions have a session tree. In **Outline → Tree**, pick an earlier message of an idle session and choose **Switch branch**: the same session continues there on a new branch, and the branch you left stays in the tree, where you can switch back to its end. Picking your own message puts it back in the composer. Branch summaries are classic only. See [Durable Sessions](docs/usage.md#durable-sessions).
