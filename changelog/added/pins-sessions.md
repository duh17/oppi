- **Client:** Pin anything a pane can show: sessions, folders and files, Godot games, a workspace's Git, commits, web pages, and extension panels. On iPad the pane menu offers **Pin** for whatever the pane shows. Sessions also pin from their row (swipe left, or touch and hold) and from **Pin Session** in a chat's **…** menu; games from the **Godot** list and the Godot pane's bar; a workspace's Git from the Git panel's bar. **Pinned** at the top of the sidebar (and the iPhone's drawer) opens a **Pinned** screen that groups pins by kind. Sessions there show their live status and open their chat; every other pin opens as its link does (on iPad, beside the chat). A pinned game shows its name and says so while Godot Pane is off. **Edit** reorders pins within each group. The screen also opens from a **Pinned** Home Screen quick action, ⇧⌘P, and `oppi://pins`. See [Usage](docs/usage.md) and [Deep links](docs/deeplinks.md).
