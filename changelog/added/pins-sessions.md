- **Client:** Sessions can be pinned: swipe left on a session row or touch and hold it and choose **Pin**, or choose **Pin Session** in a chat's **…** menu. **Pinned** at the top of the sidebar (and the iPhone's drawer) opens a **Pinned** screen with your pinned sessions, folders, and files in three groups. Sessions there show their live status and open their chat; folders and files open as before (on iPad, beside the chat). **Edit** reorders pins within each group. The screen also opens from a **Pinned** Home Screen quick action, ⇧⌘P, and `oppi://pins`. See [Usage](docs/usage.md) and [Deep links](docs/deeplinks.md).
