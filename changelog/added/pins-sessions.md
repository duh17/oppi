- **Client:** Pin anything a pane can show: sessions, folders and files, Godot games, web pages, and extension panels. On iPad the pane menu offers **Pin** for whatever the pane shows. Sessions also pin from their row (swipe left, or touch and hold) and from **Pin Session** in a chat's **…** menu; games from the **Godot** list and the Godot pane's bar. **Pinned** at the top of the sidebar (and the iPhone's drawer) opens a **Pinned** screen. Sessions come first as cards with their live status and open their chat; every other pin is a tile with a preview (a PDF's first page, a folder's items, a web page's title and image, a game's last frame and build) and opens as its link does (on iPad, beside the chat). A pinned game says so while Godot Pane is off. **Edit** removes pins and reorders them by dragging. The screen also opens from a **Pinned** Home Screen quick action, ⇧⌘P, and `oppi://pins`. See [Usage](docs/usage.md) and [Deep links](docs/deeplinks.md).
