- **Server:** A durable session's subagents pill is a composable surface now, so its drawer and its pane draw the same rows, and a failed child can be dismissed. Each row of a child that needs you offers **Dismiss**, and **Clear failed** sits beside the **Needs You** heading while two or more children fail. A dismissal hides the failure without stopping or touching the child's session: the row leaves Needs You for Recent, keeps its error line and its answer behind the fold, and stops counting time. It needs you again only when a run of that child ends after the dismissal, so a failure saved in the plugin's own document stays dismissed across a server restart. That document is also why the pill has no **Stop** on a failed, stopped child any more: a tree row has no state of its own, and the dismissal replaces that stop.
