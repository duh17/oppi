- **Client:** The sidebar, and the iPhone's drawer, have a **Pinned** section listing the server's pins above **Workspaces**, with the same breadcrumbs and **Not found** rows as the Files screen. On iPad a tapped pin opens beside the chat, and a pin drags into a pane. Touch and hold a pin to open it in a pane, rename it, or unpin it; **Edit** reorders and removes pins. The section is hidden on servers without pins, and whether it is collapsed is kept on the device. See [Usage](docs/usage.md).
