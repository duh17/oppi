- **Client:** An extension panel can show places as tiles, drawn and opened as the Pinned screen's tiles: a file's first page or picture, a folder's items, a game's last frame, a session. A tap opens the place beside the chat on an iPad, and in the chat's stack on an iPhone; a place that is gone says **Not found**. See [Tiles](docs/extension-native-ui.md#tiles).
- **Server:** A new example plugin, `example-shelf`, ships off. Its **Shelf** pill opens a panel of tiles for the files a chat wrote, its subagents, and its game. Plugins read what a session did with `oppiHost().sessionActivity`. See [Example plugins](docs/extensions.md#example-plugins).
